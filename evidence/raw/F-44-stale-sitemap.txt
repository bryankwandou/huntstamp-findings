The dead markets and the live one are the same class of response: 200, ~1.5 MB, the
application shell. Nothing distinguishes a sitemap entry that still works from one
that points at a market that no longer exists.

This differs from the invented-slug finding: there the URL was made up. Here the
site's own sitemap.xml - the file it hands to search engines - is the source of the
dead links.
